## Game Mechanics & Systems

### Core Gameplay Loop
- Player takes on the role of an **admiral** (提督, teitoku) organizing up to 4 fleets
- Combat is largely automated; manual actions include micromanagement such as building and repairing
- Progression through maps via grinding for XP, obtaining new ship girls, fulfilling quests for resources
- Acquisition of kanmusu occurs via map drops or crafting — heavily RNG-based

### Resource System (四大資源)
Four resource types that increase automatically over time:
1. **Fuel** (燃料) — fuel for ships
2. **Ammunition** (弾薬) — ammo for weapons
3. **Steel** (鋼材) — steel for construction/equipment
4. **Bauxite** (ボーキサイト) — bauxite for aircraft

Players can expeditions, sorties, and quests to further increase supplies.

### Ship Attributes (艦娘ステータス)
Combat effectiveness depends on these parameters:
- Hitpoints, Armour, Evasion
- Aircraft Capacity (for carriers)
- Speed, Attack Range, Firepower, Torpedo
- Anti-Air, Anti-Submarine, Line-of-Sight, Luck

### Leveling & Remodeling
- Ships gain XP and level up after battles
- **Remodel** (改装): ships transform into more advanced models at certain levels
- Max level: Lv.99 (base), Lv.175 (after marriage)
- **Modernization** (近代化改修, kindaika kaishu): "feed" unwanted kanmusu to another ship for attribute bonuses

### Fatigue & Sparkling System
- Ships accumulate fatigue after sorties → decreased attributes
- **Sparkling** (輝き): ships with high morale have increased attributes
- Fatigue alleviated by resting or using food supply ship Mamiya (マミヤ)
- Visual indicators: smoke icons for damage, torn clothing at low HP

### Marriage System (結婚)
- "Marriage papers and ring" item costs 700 yen or via one-time special quest
- Breaks Lv.99 cap → new max Lv.175
- Stat boosts and decreased operating costs
- Repeatable for any number of girls, no limit

### Equipment Slots & Types
Ships can be customized with equipment in empty slots:
- Naval guns, Anti-aircraft guns, Torpedoes
- Torpedo bombers, Dive bombers, Fighter aircraft
- Seaplanes, Recon planes, Radars
- Steam turbines, Special artillery shells
- Depth charges, Sonars, Drum canisters
- Searchlights, Anti-torpedo bulges

### Ship Girl Categories by Displacement
- **Larger displacement** (cruisers, battleships, carriers): depicted as young women
- **Smaller ships** (destroyers, coastal defense): depicted as pubescent girls
- Physical traits correlate to historical ship characteristics
  - e.g., Kongō adds English phrases (British origins)
  - Shimakaze is speedy girl (fastest IJN destroyer of WWII)

### Ship Roster (as of Sept 2025)
- **316 ship girls** total (810 including remodels)
- Originally all IJN, now includes foreign navies:
  Soviet Navy → Kriegsmarine → Regia Marina → US Navy → Royal Navy → French Navy → Swedish Navy → Royal Netherlands Navy → Royal Australian Navy → Republic of China Navy → Argentine Navy → Royal Norwegian Navy → Royal Thai Navy
- **32 illustrators**, **74 seiyūs**

### Server Structure (as of Jan 2016)
- 20 servers named after WWII-era Japanese naval bases
- Exercise maneuvers: PvP battles against other players' fleets
- Ranking boards with periodical prizes for top players

## Fleet Composition & Strategy

### Fleet Organization
- Up to **4 different fleets** can be organized simultaneously
- Each fleet typically has 6 ships (standard formation)
- Fleet composition depends on map requirements and enemy types

### Equipment Meta Considerations
Equipment selection is critical for combat effectiveness:
- **Radar** and **recon planes** improve line-of-sight → first strike advantage
- **Special artillery shells** provide firepower bonuses against specific targets
- **Drum canisters** increase fuel/ammo capacity for extended sorties
- **Searchlights** enable night battle engagement bonuses
- **Anti-torpedo bulges** reduce torpedo damage

### Ship Type Roles in Fleet
- **Destroyers**: fast, good torpedoes, anti-submarine screening
- **Light/Heavy Cruisers**: main firepower support, radar-equipped for spotting
- **Battleships**: heavy armor and firepower, frontline combat
- **Aircraft Carriers**: air superiority, long-range strike capability
- **Submarines**: anti-shipping, reconnaissance

### RNG & Randomization Factors
Combat outcomes involve significant randomization:
- Hit/miss calculations based on stats + RNG
- Torpedo spread patterns
- Bombing accuracy
- Equipment drop rates from crafting
- Map event triggers and enemy fleet compositions

## Game Development Timeline

### Version History
1. **1st Sequence (Flash mode)** — Original game, launched April 23, 2013
   - Discontinued August 15, 2018 (Adobe Flash EOL)
2. **2nd Sequence (HTML5 mode)** — HTML5 port with higher resolution/frame rate
3. **2nd Sequence Flight II-A** — October 17, 2025: HTTPS/SSL support, reworked fleet statistics UI

### Business Model
- Free-to-play, no gacha required for ship acquisition
- Premium items via prepaid game money and credit card microtransactions
- Premium bonuses: repair dock expansions, furniture tokens, ship limit increases, consumables
- DMM.com gained 7 billion yen from the game as of June 2014 (140x development cost)

### Current Status (as of Jan 2026)
- Game available in Japan only (interface exclusively Japanese)
- VPN required for non-Japanese players (breaches DMM terms of use)
- New player registrations: originally lottery-based, now open on server 20
- Ship roster continues to grow with seasonal events and updates

## Cultural Impact & Reception

### Player Statistics
- April 2015: **3 million registered players**
- Peak weekly spike: August 2013 — 100,000 new players in one week (600K total)
- Average spending lower than Puzzle & Dragons, Idolmaster, Love Live!

### Fandom Scale
- April 2015: **345,000+ artworks** on Pixiv tagged "KanColle" by 67,000 artists
- Niconico views: **460 million** KanColle-related videos
- Comiket 85 (2013): **1,136 dōjin circles** for KanColle (3rd behind Touhou and Kuroko's Basketball)

### Industry Recognition
- GameSpark "Online Game of the Year" 2013
- WebMoney 2013 Awards: "Rookie of the Year"
- Square Enix's Naoki Yoshida called it "Japan's answer to World of Tanks"
- Sega's Seiji Hayashi called it "innovative"

### Political Commentary
- Hankook Ilbo (Nov 2013): accused game glorifies wartime IJN
- Asahi Shimbun: unforgiving mechanics help players understand war brutality
- JMSDF collaboration: ship girls used as mascorts for real ships (e.g., JS Kaga)
