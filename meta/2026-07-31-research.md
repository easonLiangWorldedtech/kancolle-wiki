## Game Overview & Mechanics

### Basic Information
- **Full Name**: Kantai Collection (艦隊これくしょん, Kantai Korekushon; lit. 'Fleet Collection')
- **Abbreviation**: KanColle (艦これ, KanKore)
- **Developer**: Kadokawa Games (2013-2016), C2 Preparat (current operator)
- **Publisher**: DMM.com
- **Director/Designer**: Kensuke Tanaka (C2 Preparat)
- **Platforms**: HTML5 (formerly Flash, discontinued Aug 15 2018), Android (released 2016)
- **Release Date**: JP: April 23, 2013
- **Genre**: Online web browser game / Fleet raising simulation
- **Modes**: Single-player, Multiplayer (exercise maneuvers)

### Core Gameplay Loop
The gameplay centers on building squadrons composed of individual characters called "ship girls" (艦娘, kanmusu), represented as cards with different attributes. The player takes on the role of an admiral (提督, teitoku) and organizes fleets in battle to win. Combat is largely automated; manual actions include micromanagement such as building, repairing, and fleet composition decisions.

### Resource System
Construction, resupply, and repair rely on four resource types:
1. **Fuel** - consumed during sorties and expeditions
2. **Ammunition** - used for combat and equipment crafting
3. **Steel** - required for ship construction and equipment development
4. **Bauxite** - needed for aircraft operations and certain equipment

Resources gradually increase automatically over time. Expeditions, sorties, and quests provide additional resource gains.

### Fleet Organization
- Players can organize up to **four different fleets** simultaneously
- Each fleet has a specific composition based on ship types and roles
- Fleets are used for map progression, expeditions, and PvP exercise maneuvers

## Ship Girls (Kanmusu) System

### Character Design Philosophy
Ship girls are personified World War II warships depicted as teenage girls or young adult women. Physical characteristics correlate with historical displacement tonnage:
- **Larger ships** (cruisers, battleships, carriers): depicted as young women
- **Smaller ships** (destroyers, coastal defense): depicted as pubescent girls

Personality traits reflect the historical ship's history. Examples:
- Battleship Kongō adds English words/phrases due to her British origins
- Destroyer Shimakaze is portrayed as a speedy girl (she was one of WWII's fastest destroyers)

### Ship Statistics (Attributes)
Ship effectiveness depends on these attribute parameters:
1. **Hitpoints** - durability before sinking
2. **Armor** - damage reduction from attacks
3. **Evasion** - chance to dodge incoming fire
4. **Aircraft Capacity** - carrier air group size
5. **Speed** - fleet movement and engagement order
6. **Attack Range** - effective combat distance
7. **Firepower** - main gun damage output
8. **Torpedo** - torpedo attack power
9. **Anti-Air (AA)** - effectiveness against aircraft
10. **Anti-Submarine (ASW)** - anti-submarine warfare capability
11. **Line-of-Sight (LOS)** - detection range and targeting priority
12. **Luck** - critical hit chance, drop rates, morale effects

### Leveling & Progression
- Experience points gained through combat and grinding
- Maximum level: **Lv.99** (base), **Lv.175** after marriage
- Ships gain experience from battles; higher-level ships provide more XP to lower-level ones when in the same fleet

### Remodeling (改修)
Ships can be remodeled into more advanced models upon reaching certain levels, gaining:
- Improved base stats
- Additional equipment slots
- Visual design changes reflecting historical upgrades

### Modernization (近代化改修, Kindaika Kaishu)
Unwanted kanmusu can be "fed" to other ships through modernization, granting attribute bonuses in exchange for losing the sacrificed ship. This is a key progression mechanic for optimizing fleet stats.

### Marriage (結婚)
- Requires "marriage papers and ring" item (700 yen purchase or special quest reward)
- Breaks original level cap from Lv.99 to **Lv.175**
- Provides stat boosts and decreased operating costs
- Can be repeated indefinitely with no limit on number of marriages

### Fatigue System
- Ships accumulate fatigue after sorties
- Fatigued ships have **decreased attributes**
- "Sparkling" ships (high morale) have **increased attributes**
- Fatigue alleviated by resting or using food supply ship Mamiya for morale recovery
- Visual indicators: smoke from damaged ships, torn clothing at low durability

### Ship Loss
- When a ship's durability reaches zero, she is considered sunk
- Lost ships cannot be resurrected unless the player has an emergency repair item
- Players can re-train lost ships from scratch (no permanent loss of progress)

## Fleet Composition Rules & Meta Builds

### Fleet Slot Structure
Standard fleet composition follows specific slot assignments:
1. **Flagship** (旗艦) - leads the fleet, provides morale bonus
2. **DD/DE** (Destroyer/Escort Destroyer) - anti-submarine screening
3. **DD/DE** - additional destroyer slots
4. **CA/CL/TB** (Heavy Cruiser/Light Cruiser/Tokyo Express ships)
5. **CA/CL/TB** - additional cruiser slot
6. **BB/BBV/AV** (Battleship/Battle Carrier/Aircraft Carrier)
7. **CV/CVB** (Aircraft Carrier)

### Common Fleet Types & Meta Compositions

#### 1. Surface Raid Fleet (索敵型)
- Purpose: Map exploration, enemy fleet engagement
- Composition: Fast battleships + heavy cruisers + destroyers
- Key stats: Line-of-sight, firepower, speed
- Typical flagship: High-LOS ships like Myoukou class or modernized battleships

#### 2. Carrier Strike Group (空母打撃群)
- Purpose: Air superiority, long-range bombardment
- Composition: Aircraft carrier(s) + screening cruisers/destroyers
- Key stats: Aircraft capacity, AA defense, speed
- Meta carriers: Akagi, Kaga, Shoukaku, Zuikaku, Houshou (for early game)

#### 3. Anti-Submarine Screen (対潜警戒隊)
- Purpose: Submarine threat elimination, escort duty
- Composition: Destroyers with high ASW stats + sonar/drum canisters
- Key ships: Akatsuki class, Yuugure class, modernized destroyers
- Equipment focus: Type 93 torpedoes, OCA (Octuple torpedo mounts), depth charges

#### 4. Tokyo Express Fleet (東京急行)
- Purpose: Night bombardment, high-speed raids
- Composition: Heavy cruisers + destroyers (Tokyo Express ships)
- Key stats: Torpedo attack, night battle capability
- Meta ships: Myoukou class, Takao class, Aoba class

#### 5. Combined Fleet (連合艦隊)
- Purpose: Major operations, event maps
- Composition: Main fleet + escort fleet
- Requirements: Specific ship combinations and levels
- Types: Carrier combined fleet, Surface combined fleet, Submarine combined fleet

## Ship Statistics by Class (Key Meta Ships)

### Battleships (BB/BBV)
- **Yamato class**: Highest firepower and armor, slow speed
- **Nagato class**: Good all-around stats, remodel at Lv.64
- **Kongo class**: Fast battleship, English-speaking personality trait
- **Ise class**: Hybrid battleship-carrier (after remodeling)

### Aircraft Carriers (CV/CVB)
- **Akagi/Kaga**: First fleet carriers, high aircraft capacity
- **Shoukaku/Zuikaku**: Modern fleet carriers, excellent stats
- **Houshou**: Small carrier, early-game accessible
- **Taiho**: Armored carrier, designed by Fumikane Shimada

### Heavy Cruisers (CA)
- **Myoukou class**: High torpedo attack, night battle specialists
- **Takao class**: Well-rounded CA stats
- **Aoba class**: Early-game CA option

### Light Cruisers (CL/CLT)
- **Nagara class**: Good all-around CL stats
- **Sendai class**: High LOS for scouting
- **Kuma class**: Fast light cruiser, early game

### Destroyers (DD)
- **Akatsuki class**: High ASW, torpedo specialists
- **Yuugure class**: Octuple torpedo mounts available
- **Fubuki class**: First modern destroyer, good all-around stats
- **Shimakaze**: Fastest destroyer in the game

## Server & Multiplayer System

- **20 servers** available, each named after a WWII-era Japanese naval base
- PvP through exercise maneuvers menu against other players on the same server
- Ranking boards with periodical prizes for top-ranking players
- New player registration was lottery-based from August 2013 due to server overload
- Game is Japan-only; requires VPN for international play (breaches DMM ToS)

## Strategy Tips & Known Meta Builds

### Early Game Progression
1. Focus on leveling destroyers first (cheapest to build, fastest progression)
2. Use Houshou carrier for early air support
3. Prioritize resource management over aggressive sorties
4. Complete quests systematically for resource bonuses and ship drops

### Resource Management Best Practices
- Keep fuel above 500 for expedition flexibility
- Maintain steel reserves for ship construction during events
- Bauxite is critical for carrier operations - prioritize expeditions that return bauxite
- Use drum canisters on destroyers to increase torpedo capacity without sacrificing speed

### Night Battle Strategy
- Tokyo Express fleets excel at night bombardment
- High LOS ships engage first, followed by firepower-focused units
- Torpedo attacks from destroyers and cruisers are devastating in night battles
- Searchlights + spotter aircraft improve night battle targeting

### Event Map Strategies
- Combined fleet operations require specific ship combinations
- Escort missions need anti-submarine screening (destroyers with sonar)
- Resource expeditions should be prioritized during event preparation phases
- Multiple sortie attempts may be needed due to RNG-based drop mechanics
