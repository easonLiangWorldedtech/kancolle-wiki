## Game Mechanics & Systems

### Core Gameplay
- **Genre**: Online web browser game / Fleet raising simulation (艦隊育成シミュレーション)
- **Player Role**: Admiral (提督/teitoku) — organizes fleets in battle, wins through strategy
- **Combat**: Largely automated; manual actions include micromanagement such as building and repairing ships
- **Fleets**: Player can organize up to 4 different fleets simultaneously

### Ship Girls (艦娘/Kanmusu)
- **Definition**: WWII warships personified as teenage girls/young women with personality traits reflecting historical ship characteristics
- **Total Ships**: 316 ship girls (810 including remodels) as of September 2025, illustrated by 32 artists and voiced by 74 seiyūs
- **Size Convention**: Larger ships (battleships, cruisers, carriers) depicted as young women; smaller ships (destroyers, submarines) look like pubescent girls
- **Character Traits**: Based on historical ship — e.g., Kongō adds English phrases referencing British origins; Shimakaze is speedy girl since she was one of fastest WWII destroyers
- **Class Similarity**: Ships of same class/type share similar accessories or uniforms

### Ship Attributes (Combat Parameters)
12 key attributes determine combat effectiveness:
- Hitpoints, Armour, Evasion, Aircraft Capacity, Speed, Attack Range
- Firepower, Torpedo, Anti-Air, Anti-Submarine, Line-of-Sight, Luck

### Leveling & Progression
- **Base Cap**: Lv.99 for all ships
- **Marriage (結婚)**: Using marriage papers/ring item (700 yen purchase or one-time special quest) breaks level cap to Lv.175 with stat boosts and decreased operating costs; repeatable infinitely, no limit on number of marriages
- **Remodel**: Ships can be remodeled into more advanced models once reaching certain levels
- **Modernization (近代化改修/kindaika kaishu)**: Feed unwanted kanmusu to another ship for attribute bonuses — sacrifices one ship to boost another

### Resources (4 Types)
Construction, resupply and repair rely on four resources that increase automatically over time:
1. Fuel (燃料)
2. Ammo (弾薬)
3. Steel (鋼材)
4. Bauxite (ボーキサイト)

Players can engage in expeditions, sorties, and quests to further increase supplies.

### Equipment System
- Ships have empty equipment slots for customization — add attribute bonuses and special effects
- Equipment types include: Main Guns, Secondary Guns, Torpedoes, Aircraft, Depth Charges, Sonar, Radar, Special Artillery Shells, Repair Parties, Docking Machines, etc.
- New equipment can be crafted (development/開発)

### Ship Acquisition
- **Map Drops**: Random drops from enemy ships on maps — heavily RNG-based
- **Crafting**: Equipment development and ship crafting via resource investment
- **RNG Key Component**: Randomization is central to battle mechanism, map progression, and equipment development

## Development History

### Game Phases
1. **1st Sequence (Flash mode)**: Original game on Flash engine — discontinued Aug 15, 2018 when Adobe ended Flash support
2. **2nd Sequence (HTML5 mode)**: Ported to HTML5 with higher resolution and reworked elements
3. **2nd Sequence Flight II-A** (Oct 17, 2025): SSL security protocol adoption; reworked fleet statistic graphics giving player info previously only available through 3rd party viewers

### Business Model
- Unlike most DMM.com games (traditionally R-18+), KanColle designed to NOT require spending money or gacha lotteries
- Purpose: Kadokawa wanted larger target market, establish game as well-known name for merchandising and franchise works
- By Nov 2013: accounted for 30-40% of DMM.com's total online game business

### Creator Vision
Kensuke Tanaka (director/designer) previously worked on Touhou Project and other franchises. Wanted to create a game with historical elements appealing to DMM fans who enjoy mecha musume (メカ娘/Mecha girls) culture — led to anthropomorphized warship concept.
